# 🤖 ET Concierge: Video Script (3 Minutes)

**Target Audience:** Potential Users / Developers / Project Stakeholders  
**Goal:** Explain the project's purpose, key features, and technical foundation in a professional, engaging manner.

---

## 🎬 Act 1: The Problem & The Vision (0:00 - 0:45)

**[Visual: Cinematic shot of the Landing Page, scrolling past the "Empower Your Wealth" hero section]**

**Narrator:** "In today’s world, we aren't suffering from a lack of financial data—we’re suffering from a lack of *relevance*. Most financial platforms dump generic numbers and global headlines onto every user, leaving them overwhelmed.

**[Visual: Transition to the Onboarding flow]**

Enter **ET Concierge**. It’s not just another dashboard; it’s a production-grade, AI-driven financial companion designed to transform complex market noise into actionable, personal intelligence. Built on the foundation of The Economic Times ecosystem, it learns your 'Financial DNA' from the very first interaction."

**[Visual: Speed-up of the conversational onboarding answering questions about goals and risk]**

**Narrator:** "Our conversational onboarding doesn't just collect data—it builds a persona. Whether you're a 'Prudent Saver' or a 'Maverick Investor,' ET Concierge adapts its entire UI to match your goals, risk appetite, and sector interests."

---

## 🚀 Act 2: Personalized Intelligence in Action (0:45 - 1:45)

**[Visual: The main Dashboard reveals itself with a smooth fade-in]**

**Narrator:** "The Dashboard is the heart of the experience. Every morning, you're greeted with a **'Daily Action'**—a high-signal nudge generated by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash, tailored specifically to your profile.

**[Visual: Hovering over the 'Daily Brief' button and showing the audio wave animation]**

Need to catch up while on the move? The **Daily Brief** uses Text-to-Speech to generate a personalized 60-second audio briefing on market sentiment and your portfolio’s health."

**[Visual: Focus on the 'Financial Health Rings' at the top of the dashboard]**

**Narrator:** "Visual clarity is key. Our unique **Financial Health Rings** provide a real-time snapshot of your progress across Savings, Investments, Risk, and Goals, giving you a holistic view of your wealth at a single glance."

**[Visual: Smooth transition to the Markets tab, clicking on NIFTY 50 to update the technical chart]**

**Narrator:** "But we don't skimp on the data. Our **Market Intelligence** suite tracks Stocks, Forex, and Crypto in real-time. High-fidelity technical charts powered by **Recharts** allow for deep-dives into 1-day to 1-month trends, while live sparklines give you instant sentiment analysis."

---

## 🧠 Act 3: The AI Assistant & Content (1:45 - 2:30)

**[Visual: Opening the AI Concierge chat window at the bottom]**

**Narrator:** "Got a specific question like 'How do I optimize my tax shield this year?' The **24/7 AI Concierge** is always ready. It’s not a generic bot—it’s an expert advisor that understands your specific context and recommends ET services that actually fit your plan."

**[Visual: Scrolling through the 'Today's Picks' news section, highlighting the 'Why this matters' notes]**

**Narrator:** "Even our news is smart. Every headline in your feed comes with an AI-generated note explaining exactly **'Why this matters to you'**, ensuring you only spend time on what impacts your wallet."

**[Visual: Brief flash of the Admin Panel showing engagement stats and service management]**

**Narrator:** "Behind the scenes, a comprehensive **Admin Suite** allows platform managers to seed professional content, track user engagement stats, and manage featured services through a robust Supabase-backed interface."
